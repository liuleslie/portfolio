Product designer interested in complex, information-dense systems where clarity and trust are key.

Previously at Mercury, NASA JPL/Caltech, Google Labs. Based in Pacific Time.

I design for people making high-stakes decisions in information-heavy environments, such as rover operators planning drives on Mars or fintech investigators working disputes. To me, design is translation: I get up to speed quickly through immersion and conversation with experts, then turn what I learn into a map of opportunities for teams to act on. I prototype with users early, working in code whenever I can.

I’m most drawn to cybersecurity, fintech, and legal, and more broadly, any complexity worth untangling—whether it’s an operator’s dashboard or a consumer flow with a lot riding on getting the first five seconds right.

Trained across visual design and interactive media, I hold an MS in Computational Design from Carnegie Mellon, where I researched CAPTCHAs as a security measure, crowdsourcing platform, wellspring of informal economies, and aesthetic form. Before that, I spent a year researching TV dinners.

Colophon

This website is typeset in Public Sans. It was written in HTML, vanilla JS, and CSS with Sonnet 5 for layout assistance.

Background images for Hindsight are from NASA’s “Image of the Week” series from the Mars Perseverance Rover. Background images for Digital Sky Surveys are from the PanSTARRS and eROSITA surveys via AladinLite.

Drawing from the ethos of Alt Text as Poetry, HTML tags such as <details> have been kept in their brut form to reduce visual noise.
