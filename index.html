Product designer interested in complex, high-stakes systems where tacit human judgment is key. Based in Pacific Time.

I’m interested in complex, high-stakes systems where tacit human judgment is key. My background spans JPL/Caltech and Mercury, where I designed tools for/with people operating in dense, decoupled information environments. At JPL, this included rover operators correlating disconnected data sources during drive planning; at Mercury, fintech ops teams navigating fragmented internal tooling under time pressure.

Trained across visual design and interactive media, I prototype directly with end users early and often, drawing on indie web approaches to workshopping interaction patterns. At Google Labs, this meant running themed community livestreams using AI Test Kitchen tools as conversation pieces. I’m drawn to early-stage products where I can shape both product direction and interaction design directly; the domain matters less to me than the challenge of real complexity to untangle, whether it’s an operator’s dashboard or a consumer flow with a lot riding on getting the first five seconds right.

I hold a MS in Computational Design from Carnegie Mellon, where I researched CAPTCHAs as a crowdsourcing platform for data collection, wellspring of informal economies, and aesthetic form. Before this I spent a year researching TV dinners.
